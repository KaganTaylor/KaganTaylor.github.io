      🎲 Game settings

      These two numbers are shown next to the standings to remind players of this game's victory thresholds. They do not change adjudication.

      Standard: Support-hold may be given to any unit not ordered to move from its province. Support-move must exactly match another order.
      Strict support-hold: Support-hold can only be given to units ordered to hold or convoy. Units giving support cannot themselves receive support.

      Standard: An army just moves to its destination; any fleets ordered to convoy provide routes, and the move succeeds if any undisrupted route exists.
      Strict convoy: The army must name every sea it is carried through (drag it across and pick the route). The convoy succeeds only if each named sea has a fleet ordered to convoy it and none is dislodged — there are no alternate routes.
